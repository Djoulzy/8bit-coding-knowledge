## 5–10. Coverage and next investigation

The call map is selective, not a whole-program reachability graph. Build flags activate TINYNOISE, FASTMULT, SCRBUFFSAVE and six-byte scratch rows; the eight-byte alternative and disabled square-table multiply are not evidence of active optimization. The game-specific root map loader is distinct from the library's alternate loader.

No cycle totals are inferred from function names or historical comments. Resolve loaded routine addresses and conditional build identity before emulator tracing. Source tables and grammar, CRC checks, limitations and dedicated fiches are indexed in [findings](findings.md).
